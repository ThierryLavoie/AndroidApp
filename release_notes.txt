<en-US>
**What's New in Version 1.0.3**
* **Daily Challenge Improvements:** Fixed UI issues in the Clock Game where elements overlapped.
* **More Content:** Doubled the number of reading questions for both English and French!
* **Scoring Update:** Earn points for every correct answer in the Daily Challenge, plus a big bonus for 7/10!
* **Bug Fixes:** Resolved a resource issue and fixed input hints in the challenge mode.

**Previously in Ludo**
* **New Educational Games:** Master time-telling with the Clock Game, sharpen math skills with Mental Calculation (Grades 1-5), and improve literacy with Reading Practice.
* **Avatar Shop & Customization:** Earn points by playing and spend them on fun hats, glasses, and companions to personalize your avatar.
* **Progress Tracking:** Earn ranks from Novice to Grandmaster as you improve your scores.
* **Bilingual Content:** Reading modules and UI available in both English and French.
* **Privacy First:** No data collection—all your progress stays locally on your device.
